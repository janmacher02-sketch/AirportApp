# AirportReady No-Face Brand Plan

The founder does not need to show their face, post personal videos, or ask friends to test the app.

## Brand Position

AirportReady is a practical airport timing utility.

Voice:

- calm
- factual
- useful
- data-aware
- no hype

Avoid:

- founder lifestyle content
- motivational content
- generic startup content
- viral travel memes

## Brand Persona

Use the brand as the speaker:

```text
AirportReady Research
```

This allows public writing without making the founder the product.

## Content Pillars

1. Airport timing guides
   - How early to arrive at Prague Airport
   - How early to arrive at Vienna Airport

2. Security wait pages
   - Prague Airport security wait time
   - Vienna Airport security wait time

3. Travel-risk explainers
   - Why airport arrival advice changes by terminal
   - Why “arrive 2 hours early” is too generic

4. Data transparency
   - What “confidence” means in AirportReady
   - Why recent crowd reports matter

## Weekly No-Face Routine

Monday:
Create or improve one airport SEO page.

Tuesday:
Review Search Console queries and add one missing phrase to a page.

Wednesday:
Review `/admin` and note which airport has the strongest signal.

Thursday:
Improve one CTA or waitlist form.

Friday:
Write one short AirportReady Research post.

## What Success Looks Like

Early signal:

- organic page views appear
- people click Calculate
- at least one waitlist signup appears without personal outreach

Strong signal:

- organic visitors submit reports or join waitlist
- same airport gets repeated signals
- Search Console impressions grow week over week

## Founder Learning Principle

Marketing is not “posting everywhere.” Marketing is matching a painful search intent with a useful page, then measuring what users do next.
